Privacy Policy

Arrow Escape · Balkrishna Enterprise · Last updated 19 August 2026

This policy explains what happens to information when you use Arrow Escape (package com.balkrishnaenterprise.arrowescape), published by Balkrishna Enterprise.

The short version

  • We do not ask you to create an account, and we do not collect your name, email address, phone number, or location.
  • Your game progress is stored on your own device.
  • The app shows ads and supports in-app purchases. Those services are operated by Google and collect data under their own policies.

Information stored on your device

Your progress — levels completed, stars, coins and gems, streaks, unlocked themes, and your settings — is saved locally on your device so the game can resume where you left off. Deleting the app removes this data.

Information handled by third-party services

Arrow Escape includes services provided by Google. These services may collect and process data according to their own privacy policies, which we do not control:

Advertising and your choices

Where required by law, the app shows a consent screen before personalised advertising is enabled, using Google's User Messaging Platform. You can change your choice at any time from Settings → Privacy Options inside the game, where that option is available in your region.

You can also reset or delete your advertising identifier in your device settings (Settings → Google → Ads on most Android devices), which limits ad personalisation across all apps.

Removing ads

Arrow Escape offers a one-time Remove Ads purchase. After purchase, advertisements are no longer displayed. Purchase records are handled by Google Play.

Notifications

With your permission, the app may send you an occasional reminder to come back and play. Notifications are generated locally on your device — they are not sent from a server and involve no personal data. You can turn them off at any time in Settings inside the game, or in your device's notification settings.

Children

Arrow Escape is a general-audience puzzle game. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Advertising in the app is configured with a maximum content rating of PG. If you believe a child has provided personal information, please contact us and we will address it.

Data retention and deletion

Because progress is stored on your device, uninstalling Arrow Escape deletes it. To request information about data held by the third-party services listed above, please refer to their policies, or contact us and we will help where we can.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "last updated" date.
