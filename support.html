Arrow Escape · Balkrishna Enterprise

How do I play?

Tap an arrow to send it off the board. An arrow can only leave if the path in front of it is clear — so the order you tap in is the puzzle. Clear every arrow to finish the level.

I bought Remove Ads but I still see ads

Open Settings → Restore Purchases inside the game. Make sure you are signed in to the same Google account you purchased with.

I lost my progress

Progress is saved on your device. Uninstalling the app, clearing its data, or switching to a new device will not carry progress over.

How do I change my ad privacy choice?

Open Settings → Privacy Options, where that option is available in your region. You can also reset your advertising ID in your device settings.

How do I turn off reminder notifications?

Turn off Notifications in the game's Settings screen, or disable them for Arrow Escape in your device's notification settings.
